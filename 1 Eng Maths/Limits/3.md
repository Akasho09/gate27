## Area under curve :

1. The four cardioid equations

| Equation | Shape orientation |
|---|---|
| r = 2a(1 + cos θ) | Faces right |
| r = 2a(1 - cos θ) | Faces left |
| r = 2a(1 + sin θ) | Faces upward |
| r = 2a(1 - sin θ) | Faces downward |

- For all four standard cardioids,
> Area = 6 . pi . a^2
