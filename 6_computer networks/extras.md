## 1 Kbps =>> 1000 bits not 1024
- 1 Kibibit (Kib) = 1024 bits.
- Kbps, Mbps, Gbps → Multiples of 1000.
- KiB, MiB, GiB → Multiples of 1024.

## Piggybacking 
- is a technique used in bidirectional data communication in which the receiver attaches an acknowledgment (ACK) to its outgoing data frame instead of sending a separate ACK frame.
