## 4. System of linear equations
Consider the system
- AX=B
where A is the coefficient matrix, X is the vector of unknowns, and \(B\) is the constant vector.
- The most important GATE result is the     `Rouché–Capelli theorem`
- Let
- r = rank of A
- r' = rank of A or B
- n = number of unknowns
1. r!=r`	No solution
2. r = r`= n	Unique solution
3. r = r` < n	Infinitely many solutions
- Here, [A|B] denotes the augmented matrix.
