## A preposition 
- is a word used before a noun, pronoun, or gerund to show its relationship with another word in a sentence.
- It generally indicates time, place, direction, position, manner, cause, or relationship.



### 7. Rules You Must Remember for Exams
1. Do not use a preposition unnecessarily after a transitive verb.
- Incorrect: Discuss about the issue.
- Correct: Discuss the issue.

3. Use 'prefer' with 'to', not 'than'.
- Correct: I prefer tea to coffee.

4. Use 'senior', 'junior', 'superior' and 'inferior' with 'to'.
- He is senior to me.

5. We walked THROUGH the tunnel.

6. He is proficient IN several programming languages.

7. She has a preference FOR classical music over pop music.

8. Q5. The old man was oblivious TO the danger around him.
