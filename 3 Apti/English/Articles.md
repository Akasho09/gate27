## Articles 
- Articles are words used before nouns to indicate whether we are talking about something specific or general.

There are three articles in English:
1. A
2. An
3. The

- Articles are divided into two categories:
    - Indefinite Articles: A, An
    - Definite Article: The

1. Use of 'A'
- Use A before a `singular countable noun` that begins with a `consonant sound.`

Examples:
- A boy
- A girl
- A university
- A European
- A one-eyed man
- A useful book
Important: We use A based on pronunciation, not spelling.
- A university (sounds like 'yu')
- A European (sounds like 'yu')
- A one-rupee coin (sounds like 'w')

2. Use of 'An'
- Use An before a `singular countable noun` that begins with a `vowel sound.`

Examples:
- An apple
- An elephant
- An orange
- An umbrella
- An honest man
- An hour
- An MBA
- An MLA
Important: An is used according to vowel sound, not vowel letters.
For example:
- An honest man (H is silent)
- An hour (H is silent)
- An MBA (pronounced 'em-bee-ay')

3. Use of 'The'
'The' is called the definite article. It is used when we talk about a `particular or specific person, place, or thing`.

Examples:
- The boy standing there is my brother.
- The book on the table is mine.
- The sun rises in the east.

### Important Rules of Articles
1. Before unique things, use THE	
- The Sun, The Moon
2. Before rivers, use THE	
- The Ganga, The Nile
3. Before mountain ranges, use THE	
- The Himalayas
4. Before oceans and seas, use THE	
- The Pacific Ocean
5. Before newspapers, use THE	
- The Hindu, The Times of India
6. Before superlative degrees, use THE	
- The best student
7. Before ordinal numbers, use THE	
- The first chapter
8. Before musical instruments, use THE	
- Play the guitar
9. Before religious books, use THE	
- The Quran, The Bible
10. Before directions, use THE	
- The North, The South
11. Before groups of islands, use THE	
- The Andaman Islands

### When NOT to Use Articles (Zero Article)
Sometimes we do not use any article before a noun.

1. Rule 1: Before proper nouns
- Akash is a student.
- India is a beautiful country.
- Delhi is the capital of India.

2. Rule 2: Before languages
- I speak English.
- She knows Hindi.

3. Rule 3: Before subjects
- Mathematics is interesting.
- Physics is difficult.

4. Rule 4: Before meals (in general)
- I have breakfast at 8 AM.
- We had dinner together.

5. Rule 5: Before plural nouns used generally
- Dogs are loyal animals.
- Teachers play an important role.

6. Rule 6: Before uncountable nouns used generally
- Water is essential for life.
- Honesty is the best policy.

7. Rule 7: Before games and sports
- He plays cricket.
- She likes football.
